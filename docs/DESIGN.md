# Kodshim Training — game design

## Premise

You are a trainee at the Kodshim Training School, standing at the foot of the
fifteen steps that lead from the Ezras Nashim up to the Ezras Yisrael. The
kohen gadol teaches; the Levi'im play. Every stage you complete carries you up
one step. On the fifteenth you stand at the gate with the whole ensemble
behind you.

## Structure: five levels, fifteen steps

The test bank (`kodshim-practice.csv`) holds five levels. Each level is three
stages, and each stage is one step on the staircase:

| Stage  | What it is                                                   | Pass bar                    |
|--------|--------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Learn  | Lessons: short explanations, term cards, tables, sources     | Reach the end               |
| Prove  | 12 cards drawn from this level's bank, three hearts          | 70 %, hearts left           |
| Master | 12 cards: 8 from this level weighted toward your misses, 4 from earlier levels | 85 %, hearts left |

- 5 levels × 3 stages = 15 steps.
- Level n+1 opens when level n is **proved**. Mastery is optional but is what
  moves you up the last step of each level and unlocks the next instrument.
- Stars: 3 for a perfect run with all hearts, 2 for a pass with two hearts,
  1 for any pass. Prove and Master each hold their own stars (max 6 per level).
- XP: 10 per correct answer, bonuses for first pass and new stars. It is a
  number on the map, nothing more.

### Instruments

Same five tracks as before, layered. Nevel plays from the start; kinnor joins
at step 3 (level 1 mastered), chalil at 6, chatzotzra at 9, tziltzel at 12.
At step 15 the shofar sounds.

## Review (spaced repetition)

Every quiz card has a Leitner box (0–5). Right moves it up one box; wrong
drops it to 0. Boxes map to intervals of 0, 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days. The Review
tab shows how many cards are due across all learned levels and runs a
no-hearts session on them (due cards first, then the weakest). Prove and
Master runs also feed the boxes, so the picker leans toward cards you have
missed.

## Question kinds

| kind   | Interaction                                      |
|--------|--------------------------------------------------|
| mc     | pick one of 3–4 options                          |
| tap    | tap the right Hebrew word                        |
| match  | tap a Hebrew term, then its meaning              |
| sort   | drag chips into 2–3 buckets, then Check          |
| tf     | "Kosher or pasul?" — true / false                |
| cloze  | pick the word that completes the halacha         |

After every answer a feedback card shows the correct answer, a one-or-two
sentence explanation, and the source. The player presses Continue (no timer).
A streak counter rewards runs of correct answers.

## Content

- `web-app/content/level-N.js` per level, schema in `CONTENT-SCHEMA.md`.
  Level 1 keeps its bespoke illustrated slides; levels 2–5 use the generic
  lesson renderer (cards / table / compare / callout / steps / list / terms /
  figure blocks, revealed one block per click).
- `web-app/kodshim-sources.js` is generated by `tools/build_sources.py`:
  Hebrew text for every cited Rambam halacha and Vayikra verse from
  `source-material/`, English from `tools/translations.json`
  (produced once by `tools/translate_sources.py` through OpenRouter).
- Lessons and quiz items cite with strings like `Maaseh HaKorbanot 1:14`;
  the sources drawer resolves them.

## Tutor

"Ask the kohen" opens a side drawer on any Learn, Prove or Review screen.
It calls OpenRouter directly from the browser with a key the player pastes
into Settings (stored only in that browser's localStorage). The system
prompt carries the current level's concepts and source translations, so
answers stay inside the material. From a feedback card, "Explain this"
pre-fills the question.

## Persistence

`localStorage`:
- `kodshim.progress.v2` — per-level stage results, Leitner boxes, XP.
- `kodshim.settings.v2` — music, sound effects, mixer volumes, tutor key and model.

Settings has a Reset progress button.

## Screens

1. Welcome (unchanged art) → Tutorial card (rewritten for the three stages).
2. **Map**: the staircase with 15 steps, the avatar on the current step,
   stars per level, due-review badge, XP, instruments lit. Tap a level for its
   card: tagline, stages with stars, Learn / Prove / Master / Review buttons.
3. Learn (bespoke for level 1, generic for 2–5) with the sources drawer.
4. Prove / Master / Review runs share one quiz screen.
5. Result / Success with stars, XP earned, instrument unlock, next action.
6. Settings and About modals from the map toolbar.
